Good to know

Answers before you ask

How fast is the delivery?

24 to 48 hours as standard. Same-day express is available if we collect before 11am, for 40% more on the order value.

Is pickup really free?

Yes, on every order, with no minimum value, anywhere in Daliganj and the nearby areas of Lucknow.

What if something is damaged?

Tell us within 24 hours of delivery. If the fault is ours we re-do the piece free, or settle it fairly.

How do I pay?

Cash on delivery, UPI or card. Monthly plan customers are billed once at the start of the month.

Not sure which service you need?

Call us and describe the garment — we will tell you the honest, cheapest option.
