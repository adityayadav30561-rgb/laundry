How we work

A small shop that runs like a workshop

Every order is tagged with your name and counted in front of you. Garments are separated by fabric and colour before anything touches water.

Stains are treated by hand, delicate pieces cleaned separately, and everything steam pressed and packed before it goes back on the delivery bike. If something is not right, we re-do it free.
