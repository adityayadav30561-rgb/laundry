The laundry your neighbours already trust

Aarika Fabric Care opened in Daliganj to solve one small, daily problem: clothes that come back late, faded or badly pressed. We built the shop around care instead of speed alone.

A small shop that runs like a workshop

Every order that comes in is tagged with your name and counted in front of you. Garments are then separated by fabric and colour before anything touches water.

Stains are treated by hand, delicate pieces are cleaned separately, and everything is steam pressed and packed before it goes back on the delivery bike. If something is not right, we re-do it free.
