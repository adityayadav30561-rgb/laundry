Every file here was checked to be a free Unsplash photo, not an Unsplash+
one. The paid ones sit behind the same search results and refuse to download
without a subscription, so anything that did not download cleanly was
dropped rather than worked around. Each was also looked at before use: several
otherwise fine photographs were rejected for showing a coin laundromat, which
is the wrong business, or for being too dim and cramped to represent this one.

The shirts in services-hero.jpg were cropped in from the right to take a
garment maker's brand label out of frame.

logo-full.png is Aarika's own logo, cropped from the business's poster artwork.

Replacing one: keep the filename, drop the new file in, and update the width
and height attributes on the matching <img> tag so the page does not shift
while it loads. Every slot uses object-fit: cover, so keep the subject centred.
